# Independent review of the elementary reductions

**Subsequent status:** the remaining elementary obligations described in
this review have now been discharged, including parabolic laws, matrix
lengths and full-to-partial convolution normalization. The final web bundle
has only `refinement` and `full_flag` fields: Schur support is derived from
refinement. See [audit-input-necessity.md](audit-input-necessity.md),
[audit-elementary-inputs.md](audit-elementary-inputs.md) and the current
`literature-inputs.md`; the review below records its earlier scope.

30 October 2026. This review concerns the reductions in `KLInversion.lean`,
`FiniteSymmetrizerEigen.lean`, `FiniteSymmetrizerAbsorption.lean`,
`SphericalMergeConstruction.lean`, and their use by `ParabolicWeb.lean`,
`KLFamily.lean`, and `PolynomialWebLiterature.lean`. It checks the meanings of
the objects and hypotheses, in addition to the successful Lean checks.
The final project-wide verification report remains the authority on the
integrated build and its full list of declarations.

## Findings

The inspected reductions prove the former fields from the retained
hypotheses. They do not replace the presented Hecke algebra, change either
basis, assume the desired family coefficient, or replace the spherical
module by a numerical encoding.

1. **Ordinary KL inversion.** The standard basis still consists of the
   specified reduced words in the quotient by the quadratic and braid
   relations. `reverseMap` is constructed by reindexing this actual basis
   by group inversion. Its interaction with a generator is proved using
   the two Coxeter length cases and the quadratic relation. Its interaction
   with bar is then proved on words and extended by basis reconstruction.
   Canonical uniqueness gives the canonical inversion identity, and the
   injectivity of the existing normalization recovers the ordinary KL
   polynomial identity. No polynomial-inversion hypothesis remains in
   `Hecke.Theory`; its `inversion` method invokes this proof. Inversion
   preserves the lower endpoint because the finite longest element is
   self-inverse, as checked in the existing family transfer.

2. **Generator eigenvalues.** `FiniteSymmetrizerEigen.sum` is an actual
   finite sum of standard Hecke basis vectors with coefficients
   `v^(d-length(w))`. Left multiplication permutes the actual subgroup
   indices. The ascent/descent calculation proves eigenvalue `v^-1`, with
   exactly the convention `(H_s-v^-1)(H_s+v)=0`. Right eigenvalues follow
   from the constructed reversal map and the inverse permutation of the
   subgroup. The arbitrary integer `d` is retained throughout. In
   `ParabolicWeb.Laws`, these statements are instantiated using the
   retained `standard_sum` field; the result has not been proved without
   that field at this review stage.

3. **Absorption.** The closure generated by the simple reflections agrees
   with their monoid closure because each generator is its own inverse.
   This justifies induction without assuming that a parabolic element has
   a reduced expression in the parabolic generators. Each induction step
   is proved in both Coxeter length cases. Multiplying the actual finite
   sum gives the scalar `sum_w v^(d-2 length(w))`. For nested parabolics,
   the already proved generator eigenvalues provide precisely the needed
   hypothesis. Thus the former absorption field is now a consequence of
   the unchanged standard-sum field and the actual Hecke relations.

4. **Integral merges.** `spherical C` remains the submodule of the actual
   algebra consisting of all products `C*h`. Its generator map is
   surjective. From `C_P*C_L=d*C_P` and injectivity of scalar multiplication
   by `d`, the new proof establishes the required containment of generator
   kernels. It then descends through the quotient by the smaller kernel.
   The resulting linear map sends every `C_L*h` to `C_P*h`, not just one
   chosen vector. In the Hecke application the scalar is the same finite
   Poincare sum, proved nonzero by evaluation at `v=1`; cancellation comes
   from the actual Hecke basis. No scalar is inverted and no integral
   divisibility assertion is substituted for a constructed map. The
   existing six-map composite and its cleared identity consequently keep
   their original meaning and order of factors.

5. **Final wiring.** `PolynomialWebLiterature.toWebLiterature` supplies
   its longest data from `ElementaryCoxeterParabolic.longestData`, finite
   source reversal from `ElementaryCoxeterFinite.standardFiniteLongest`,
   finite-vector-space counts from `GeneralLatticeFlagInput.proved`, all
   four polynomial orbit-coordinate formulas from
   `PolynomialOrbitCounting.provedInput`, and diagram invariance from
   `ElementaryCoxeterDiagram.diagramInvariance`. These are constructions
   used by the final route, rather than additional theorems left unused.
   The completed-lattice inputs are still obtained by the previously proved
   model comparisons. The final named family, fixed ordinary lower
   endpoint, characteristic bounds, and absolute-boundedness statement
   in `ModelComparisonCounterexample` are unchanged.

`CanonicalLengthFiltration.lean` supplies a further elementary consequence:
standard and canonical coordinates define the same length filtration, and
agree at the top permitted length. The proof uses a maximal index in the
finite canonical support and subword triangularity. It introduces no
positivity or additional basis hypothesis. This review does not count that
lemma as an independent verification by a second author: it was written by
the reviewer.

## Remaining scope

The reduction of a structure field to a theorem does not by itself prove
all of the remaining fields. At this review stage the following boundary
must remain explicit:

- The finite canonical symmetrizer standard sum and the integral inclusion
  for nested spherical ideals are still fields of `ParabolicWeb.Laws`.
  Eigenvalues, absorption, and merge construction do not alone discharge
  them.
- The polynomial convolution specialization still packages the major
  affine flag/Hecke convolution theorem with elementary complete-to-partial
  flag fiber division and the exponent `chain.weight-length(w)`. Closing
  that elementary part requires a source-level full-flag realization and
  proofs of its actual lift fibers and map compositions. Merely renaming
  the existing chain formula would leave the same obligation.
- The arbitrary cyclic block-length deduction and elementary
  orbit-length consequences are being completed in separate modules.
  Their subsequent integration must be checked against the final
  structure fields; this review does not prejudge that build.
- The general Coxeter/KL, positivity, Schur, orbit-dimension, and
  representation-theoretic theorems remain explicit mathematical
  hypotheses. The statement about `#print axioms` is a statement about
  Lean's foundational axioms, not evidence that those theorem arguments
  have been instantiated unconditionally.

The inspected source contains no new axioms, `sorry`, `admit`, or compiled
proof oracle. The reductions support the same conditional counterexample;
they do not yet justify the claim that every remaining input is only a
major published theorem.
