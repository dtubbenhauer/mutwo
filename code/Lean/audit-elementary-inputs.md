# Elementary deductions and the external-theorem boundary

30 October 2026. This audit applies the user's stricter requirement: major
published theorems may remain explicit hypotheses; elementary deductions
and changes of convention must be proved. The previous boundary grouped
some routine consequences with their framework theorems. That was too
broad for this requirement. A generic statement is not necessarily a major
theorem, and attaching a citation does not discharge its proof obligation.

verified arithmetic on a matrix is not substituted for its mathematical

## Boundary after the reductions

The elementary obligations identified in this pass have been proved and
wired into the final theorem. `PolynomialWebLiterature` now has exactly
two fields: `refinement` and `full_flag`. The retained standard-span input
now supplies canonical corner support as well as coordinate ratios. Its
methods construct the former parabolic laws, matrix-length dictionary,
coordinate formulas, flag counts, block lengths, invariance and partial-chain convolution.
`full_flag` contains only actual full-flag orbit classification and binary
Hecke convolution, not the former assembled formula. See
[audit-full-flag-normalization.md](audit-full-flag-normalization.md).

Bar involutivity and nontriviality of the actual family action are now
proved, and the unused Andersen finiteness conjunct is removed. The
necessity audit therefore reduces the earlier eleven named packages to ten:
three Hecke packages, two web packages, and five representation packages.
That count does not assert logical independence or minimality; see
[audit-input-necessity.md](audit-input-necessity.md).

The original-source Andersen convention check remains a documented
source-access qualification. The remaining literature theorems are ordinary
explicit hypotheses; their citations do not instantiate them in Lean.

## Major external mathematics

The genuine framework dependencies are the affine Coxeter realization,
existence of the ordinary Hecke/KL bases, Elias--Williamson positivity,
canonical/standard Schur-span compatibility, full-flag orbit classification
and binary affine Hecke convolution, highest-weight theory, Andersen/AJS, the regular
cohomology interpretation, BNP comparison, Steinberg restricted irreducibility and PSL
simplicity. Their exact formulations and checked source locations remain
in `literature-inputs.md`. Their presence is separate from the elementary deductions proved above.

## Verification and examples

All completed proofs use kernel checking. No new axioms, `sorry`, `admit`,
`native_decide` or compiled proof oracle is used. `AxiomAudit.lean` checks
principal declarations, and `scripts/verify.py` builds every source, checks
pins and import coverage, regenerates the original certificates and checks
the manuscript hash. The pre-reduction successful report is preserved as
`reports/pre-elementary-reduction-verification.json`. The earlier elementary
pass checked 232 Lean sources and 265 principal declarations, with only
the standard foundational axioms. The current integrated result is recorded
in `reports/verification.json`; source and check counts there also include
the subsequent necessity reductions. The original certificates and
unchanged manuscript remain part of that verification.

Examples include zero and invalid flag intervals, seven lines and seven
planes and twenty-one incident flags over F2^3, empty/reversed row intervals,
signed Laurent cuts, and the one-dimensional ambient case. These are checks
of the uniform proofs, not finite-range substitutes for them.
