\section{A question about growth}
The parabolic endpoint $\beta_m$ is the last member of our row. For each
sufficiently large prime $p$, the modules in the row have first cohomology
dimensions $1,\ldots,m$ for $\mathrm{PSL}_{2m+1}(\mathbb F_p)$, of Lie rank $2m$.
Can one find a family for
which the first cohomology dimension grows faster than linearly with the rank?
We would be glad to explore this question together.
